Problem 1: 


A college wants to maintain a list of students.

Requirements
Add a new student.
Add multiple students at once.
Insert a student at a specific position.
Remove a student by name.
Remove the last student.
Search for a student.
Count how many times a student name appears.
Find the index of a student.
Sort students alphabetically.
Reverse the student list.
Create a backup of the list.
Clear the entire list.
Display total students.
Find duplicate names.
Remove duplicate names.

append()
extend()
insert()
remove()
pop()
index()
count()
sort()
reverse()
copy()
clear()
